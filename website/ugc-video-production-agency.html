Our strategy

We use a hook → problem → solution → CTA framework that's been battle-tested across 100+ accounts. Every video starts with a 3-second hook variation pulled from a tested library. We A/B test hooks ruthlessly — same product, same offer, different hook can mean 3× the conversion.

We produce in batches (10-15 videos per shoot day) and ship 8-12 variants per week into your ad account. Winners get scaled, losers get retired within 5 days. No video lives in a vault.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UGC video production?

UGC (User Generated Content) video production is the creation of authentic, creator-led video content — typically filmed in selfie format with handheld cameras or phones — that's used for paid social advertising. UGC converts 3× better than polished studio ads because it feels real, not branded.

How many UGC videos do I need per month?

For a brand running Meta Ads at scale, we recommend 32-48 new video variants per month (8-12 per week). The Meta algorithm rewards creative variety — brands shipping fewer videos consistently underperform.

How much do UGC videos cost in India?

UGC video production in India ranges from ₹3,000 per video for solo creator UGC to ₹15,000+ for fully produced video with multiple hooks and B-roll. Brandzoo Media packages cost between ₹40,000-₹2,00,000 per month based on volume.

Do you provide creators or do we cast our own?

We have an in-house creator network of 14+ vetted creators across India covering different demographics and verticals. We cast based on your brand fit. If you want to use your own creators, we can also work with that — scripting, shooting direction, and editing.

Can UGC work for B2B brands?

Yes. B2B UGC typically takes the form of founder-led video, customer testimonials, and product walkthroughs. The same hook-led principles apply — open with curiosity, demonstrate value, drive to action.

Who owns the rights to the UGC videos?

You do, fully — for paid and organic usage. We include usage rights as part of every contract. Creators are paid for usage rights upfront so there are no ongoing fees.
