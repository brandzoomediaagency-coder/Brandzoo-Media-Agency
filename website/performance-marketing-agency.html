FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is performance marketing?

Performance marketing is paid advertising where you only pay for measurable outcomes — leads, sales, app installs, or qualified clicks. Unlike traditional brand advertising that optimizes for reach or impressions, performance marketing is engineered around return on ad spend (ROAS), cost per acquisition (CPA), and contribution margin.

How is performance marketing different from regular digital marketing?

Regular digital marketing includes a mix of brand, content, social, and paid. Performance marketing is the subset focused specifically on direct-response paid media with strict ROAS targets. At Brandzoo Media we run both — but performance is where we measure success in revenue, not vanity metrics.

What's a good ROAS for an e-commerce business?

A break-even ROAS depends on your contribution margin. For most D2C brands with 60-70% gross margin, a profitable ROAS sits between 2.5x and 4x. Premium products with higher margins can profitably scale at 2x. Low-margin products often need 5x+ to be sustainable.

Do you run Meta Ads or Google Ads or both?

Both. We're platform-agnostic — we pick the channel mix that fits your data, product, and audience. Most D2C accounts run 60/40 Meta/Google. Lead-gen businesses often skew 70/30 Google/Meta. We build the right mix from your unit economics, not platform preference.

Can you take over an existing ad account?

Yes. About 70% of new clients come with existing Meta and Google accounts. We start with a deep account audit, restructure for algorithmic learning, fix tracking gaps, and ship new creative — usually within the first 2 weeks.

What's the minimum ad spend you work with?

We work best with brands spending ₹2L+ per month on ads. Below that, the management fee becomes inefficient. If you're below that threshold, we'll be honest with you and recommend either freelancers or a self-managed approach with our consulting support.
