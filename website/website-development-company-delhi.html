Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Delhi?

Marketing sites: ₹60K-₹4L. Mid-complexity e-commerce: ₹1.5L-₹10L. Custom Next.js/headless: ₹3L-₹25L+. Prices depend on scope, integrations, and stack — we quote fixed prices, not hourly.

How long does a Delhi website project take?

Marketing sites: 3-5 weeks. E-commerce: 6-10 weeks. Custom Next.js: 8-16 weeks. Timeline depends on content readiness, integration complexity, and approval cycles.

Do you also handle SEO for the new website?

Yes. Every site we ship has SEO baked in (schema, semantic HTML, fast load, mobile-perfect, sitemap, robots). For ongoing SEO including content and link building, see our SEO services in Delhi.

Can you migrate my existing Delhi business website?

Yes. We've migrated sites from WordPress to Shopify, Wix to Next.js, and legacy custom stacks to modern frameworks. Migrations include URL redirects, SEO preservation, content port, and post-launch indexation monitoring.

Do you provide post-launch website maintenance in Delhi?

Yes. Monthly retainers cover bug fixes, content updates, performance monitoring, security patches, and feature additions. Project-based clients get 30 days of free post-launch support.
