FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a digital marketing agency in Delhi do?

A digital marketing agency in Delhi like Brandzoo Media handles all the online channels that drive growth — paid ads on Meta and Google, search engine optimization, social media marketing, content and UGC video production, lead generation funnels, and website development. We build and run these as one connected system rather than isolated services.

How much does digital marketing cost in Delhi?

Digital marketing retainers in Delhi typically range from ₹40,000 to ₹5,00,000+ per month depending on services scope, ad spend, and the complexity of your funnel. Brandzoo Media offers month-to-month plans starting at ₹40,000 with no long-term lock-ins.

How long before I see results from digital marketing?

Paid ads start delivering leads within 1-2 weeks of launch. SEO typically shows traction in 2-3 months and compounds heavily after month 4. Our 90-day plans are designed to deliver tangible KPI improvements (CPL, ROAS, traffic) within the first quarter.

Do you only work with clients in Delhi?

No. We're based in New Delhi but serve clients across India (Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune) and globally (UAE, US, UK, Singapore). All work is delivered remotely with weekly calls and live dashboards.

Can you handle both website development and marketing under one roof?

Yes — that's actually our differentiator. We build SEO-ready, Core Web Vitals-optimised websites and then run Meta & Google Ads against them. Same team, same stack, faster results.

Do you guarantee results?

No serious agency can guarantee specific numbers — anyone who does is misleading you. What we do guarantee is a clear plan, weekly transparent reporting, senior-led execution, and the ability to walk away any month if we're not delivering.
