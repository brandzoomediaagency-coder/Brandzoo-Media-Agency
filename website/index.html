Commonly Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scaling your brand with Brandzoo Media.

What is Performance Marketing, and how does it differ from traditional ads?

Performance marketing focuses on measurable results—like leads, sales, or clicks—rather than just brand awareness. You pay primarily when a specific action is completed, ensuring your budget is spent on actual outcomes.

How can UGC Videos improve my social media conversion?

UGC (User Generated Content) feels more organic and trustworthy. Statistics show that UGC videos can increase conversion rates by up to 30% compared to traditional studio-produced ads because they build genuine rapport with the audience.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

While some technical optimizations show impact immediately, sustainable organic growth typically takes 3 to 6 months. Our AI-powered SEO strategies often accelerate this timeline by identifying low-hanging fruit keywords faster.
