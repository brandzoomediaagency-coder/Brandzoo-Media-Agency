FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with Brandzoo Media.

What is Performance Marketing and how is it different from regular advertising?

Performance marketing focuses on measurable outcomes — leads, sales, or qualified clicks — instead of just impressions. You only invest when your campaigns deliver real business actions, ensuring every rupee of your budget is tied to a concrete result. We run Google Ads, Meta Ads, Performance Max, and full-funnel paid media optimized for ROAS, not vanity.

How can UGC Videos increase my sales?

UGC (User Generated Content) videos feel authentic and trustworthy. They convert up to 3× better than traditional studio ads because consumers trust real people over polished brand ads. Our team handles end-to-end UGC production: casting, scripting, filming, editing, distribution, and creative testing.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Technical SEO wins can show within weeks. Sustainable organic traffic typically builds over 3–6 months. Our AI-powered SEO strategies target quick-win keywords first to accelerate the timeline while we build long-term authority.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Absolutely. We offer customized retainers for startups, SMEs, and enterprise brands. Whether you need full-service growth or just Meta Ads, we tailor scope and pricing to your stage and goals.

Which industries do you specialize in?

We've shipped winning campaigns in e-commerce, real estate, healthcare, EdTech, SaaS, B2B services, fashion, finance, restaurants, and local businesses. Our system adapts to any vertical.

Do you build websites too — or only run ads?

Both. We build SEO-ready, lightning-fast websites and landing pages (WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, React) — then run Meta & Google Ads against them. Same team. Same stack. Faster results.

How is "AI Visibility" different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO targets Google's blue links. AI Visibility ensures your brand gets cited inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity answers. We structure your content, schema, and authority signals so AI models actually quote your site as a source.
