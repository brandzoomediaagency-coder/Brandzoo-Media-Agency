FAQ

Commonly Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scaling your brand with Brandzoo Media.

What is Performance Marketing and how is it different from regular advertising?

Performance marketing focuses on measurable outcomes — leads, sales, or clicks — instead of just brand awareness. You pay primarily when a specific action is completed, ensuring every rupee of your budget delivers a concrete result. At Brandzoo Media, we run Google Ads, Meta Ads, and PPC campaigns fully optimized for conversions.

How can UGC Videos increase my sales?

UGC (User Generated Content) videos feel authentic and trustworthy — consumers trust real people over polished brand ads. Studies show UGC increases conversion rates by up to 30%. Our team handles end-to-end UGC production: casting, scripting, filming, editing, and distribution.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Technical SEO improvements can show impact within weeks, but sustainable organic traffic typically builds over 3–6 months. Our AI-powered SEO strategies identify quick-win keywords and high-authority opportunities faster, accelerating your timeline.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Absolutely. We have customized packages for startups, SMEs, and enterprise brands. Whether you need a full-service digital strategy or just performance ads, we tailor our approach to your budget and goals.

Which industries does Brandzoo Media specialize in?

We have successfully delivered campaigns across e-commerce, fashion, food & beverage, healthcare, real estate, EdTech, SaaS, and local businesses. Our AI-first approach adapts to any industry vertical.
