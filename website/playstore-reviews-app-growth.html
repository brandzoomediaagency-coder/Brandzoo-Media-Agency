FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does Brandzoo improve Playstore reviews ethically?

We use Google's official In-App Review API, which prompts users at retention-optimized moments (e.g., after a successful purchase, milestone, or 3+ session days). Combined with in-app feedback loops, this can lift ratings from 3.4★ to 4.7★ within 60-90 days — all 100% Google-compliant.

What is ASO (App Store Optimization)?

ASO is the practice of optimizing an app's Playstore/App Store listing to rank higher in category and keyword searches. Includes title, description, screenshots, preview video, ratings, and review keywords. ASO can drive 30-60% of total install volume.

How much does app marketing cost?

Retainers range from ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000 per month + ad spend. Pricing depends on attribution platform (Appsflyer/Adjust costs separately), creative volume, and campaign complexity. UAC ad spend typically starts at ₹2-5L per month for meaningful learning.

Do you handle both iOS and Android?

Yes — both Playstore and App Store. ASO and creative differ between platforms; we optimize separately for each store while keeping campaign strategy unified.

How long until I see install volume?

UAC campaigns start delivering installs within 1-2 weeks. Meaningful retention data emerges by month 2. By month 4-6 you should see 3-5× install volume growth on a well-executed engagement.

Do you handle iOS App Store reviews too?

Yes. We use Apple's official SKAdsAttribution APIs and in-app review prompts. iOS review strategy is similar in principle but more restricted in execution (timing limits, no incentivized reviews).
