Our strategy

Our Delhi Google Ads playbook starts with neighborhood-level keyword research — finding what Delhi residents actually search (‘dentist in CP’, ‘PG in Karol Bagh’, ‘wedding photographer South Delhi’). We then build campaigns segmented by intent (high vs. consideration) and geography (Delhi central, South, West, North, East, Gurgaon, Noida).

Every campaign is paired with location-aware ad copy, landing pages with NAP + Google Map embed + GBP link, and Local Service Ads where applicable. Combined with GBP optimization, you dominate both paid and organic local SERPs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Google Ads in Delhi different from national campaigns?

Delhi Google Ads campaigns require neighborhood-level keyword research, location-aware ad copy, tight radius targeting (typically 3-10 km), Local Service Ads where eligible, and GBP integration. Landing pages need NAP, embedded maps, and trust signals specific to the locality.

Do you handle Local Service Ads (LSA)?

Yes — for eligible categories (lawyers, plumbers, electricians, locksmiths, real estate, financial services). LSAs are pay-per-lead and appear above standard Search ads with the Google Guaranteed badge.

Which Delhi areas do you cover?

All of Delhi NCR: New Delhi (Central, South, West, North, East), Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad. We optimize by neighborhood when that level of granularity makes sense.

Can you target both Delhi and other Indian cities?

Yes. Most clients run a Delhi-primary campaign plus expansion campaigns for Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune. We structure accounts by geography so reporting and bid management stay clean.

How fast do Google Ads results show in Delhi?

Search campaigns deliver leads within the first 7-14 days. Local Service Ads typically show first leads within 3-5 days after approval. PMax needs 4-6 weeks of conversion data to optimize.
