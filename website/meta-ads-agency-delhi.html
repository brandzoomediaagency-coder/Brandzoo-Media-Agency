FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you produce Hindi/English ad creative for Delhi audiences?

Yes. Most Delhi NCR audiences respond best to bilingual (Hindi + English) creative. We script, produce, and edit UGC video in both languages and test which performs better for each audience segment.

Can Meta Ads target specific Delhi neighborhoods?

Yes. Meta allows geo-targeting down to 1km radius around any location. We segment campaigns by major Delhi areas (South Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, etc.) when audience behavior differs between regions.

How do click-to-WhatsApp ads work?

Click-to-WhatsApp ads send users directly into a pre-populated WhatsApp conversation with your business. We set up automated welcome flows and lead qualification, so leads arrive pre-qualified. CPL is typically 30-50% lower than form leads.

Do you handle both Facebook and Instagram?

Yes — Meta Ads cover both platforms (same Ads Manager). We optimize creative for Reels, Stories, Feed, and the broader Meta network. Most Delhi audiences over-index on Instagram Reels and Stories.

What budget should I start with?

Minimum ₹50,000/month for meaningful learning. Lead-gen sweet spot: ₹1L-3L/month. D2C scale: ₹3L+/month. Below ₹50K we recommend either consulting or hiring an in-house junior — agency overhead doesn't make sense.
