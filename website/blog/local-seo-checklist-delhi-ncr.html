Local SEO Checklist for Businesses in Delhi NCR

Use this checklist to audit your local SEO. Most Delhi businesses score 30-50%. Reaching 80%+ usually doubles map-pack visibility within 90 days.

Google Business Profile (GBP) basics

The most important asset for any Delhi local business. Check each item:

  • Profile fully claimed and verified
  • Primary category accurately set
  • 2-3 secondary categories added
  • Business name matches exactly across web (NAP consistency)
  • Phone number and address exactly match website
  • Hours of operation accurate (including special hours)
  • Service areas defined (if applicable)
  • All services listed individually (not bulk text)
  • Service descriptions completed (each ~250 characters)
  • Logo + cover photo uploaded
  • 10+ business photos uploaded (interior, exterior, team, products)
  • Q&A section seeded with common questions and answers
  • Posts published weekly (offers, updates, events)
  • Reviews responded to within 24 hours
  • Attributes complete (wheelchair access, parking, etc.)

On-page SEO essentials

  • Unique title tags per page (50-60 characters, include location)
  • Unique meta descriptions per page (140-160 characters)
  • One H1 per page matching primary keyword
  • Logical H2/H3 hierarchy
  • Internal links between related pages
  • Image alt text on all images
  • Clean URLs (no /page?id=123 nonsense)
  • HTTPS enabled and forced
  • XML sitemap submitted to Search Console
  • robots.txt allowing all important pages
  • Canonical tags on all pages

Local content essentials

  • Homepage mentions city + neighborhoods served
  • Dedicated ‘Service in [City]’ pages for each service-location combo
  • About page mentions city, year founded, team
  • Contact page includes embedded Google Map
  • NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistent in footer of every page
  • Schema markup for LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb
  • Photos with descriptive alt text (e.g., ‘Dental clinic interior in South Delhi’)
  • Blog posts targeting local intent (e.g., ‘Best dentist in Hauz Khas’)

Local citation building

List your business on these directories with consistent NAP:

Tier 1 (must-have)

  • Google Business Profile
  • Bing Places
  • Justdial
  • Sulekha
  • Apple Maps
  • Facebook Page
  • LinkedIn Company Page
  • Yelp India

Tier 2 (industry-specific)

  • Practo (healthcare)
  • Tripadvisor (hospitality)
  • UrbanCompany (services)
  • NoBroker / 99acres (real estate)
  • IndiaMART (B2B)
  • Zomato (F&B)

Tier 3 (general directories)

  • IndiaCompanies
  • YellowPages India
  • HotFrog India
  • India Online

Reviews strategy

  • Review velocity: 5-15+ new reviews per month
  • Average rating: 4.5★+ minimum to compete
  • Response rate: 100% of reviews (positive and negative) within 24 hours
  • Diverse review sources (not all on Google)
  • Photos in reviews (Google weighs these higher)
  • Specific service mentions in reviews (helps SEO)
  • Review request flow built into post-purchase emails / WhatsApp

Technical SEO for local

  • Page speed (LCP < 1.5s on mobile)
  • Mobile responsiveness (Core Web Vitals green)
  • HTTPS secure
  • Structured data (Schema.org) validated for LocalBusiness
  • Open Graph + Twitter Card tags
  • Sitemap submitted to Search Console
  • No duplicate content across location pages
  • 404 errors monitored and fixed
  • Redirects in place for old URLs
  • Search Console connected and monitored monthly

Local backlinks (white-hat only)

  • Local newspaper mentions (Times of India, Hindustan Times, NDTV)
  • Industry blog guest posts
  • Local event sponsorships with backlink
  • Charity / community organization links
  • Partner business cross-links
  • Local podcast appearances
  • Founder interview features

Avoid: Paid link packages, PBNs, link farms, low-quality directory spam. These will hurt rankings long-term.

Tracking and reporting

  • Google Analytics 4 connected and configured
  • Search Console connected (both web and Google Business Profile)
  • Conversion tracking set up (calls, form submissions, WhatsApp clicks)
  • Rank tracking for primary local keywords
  • Monthly review of: organic traffic, ranking positions, GBP insights (calls, direction requests, website clicks), conversion volume

Score yourself

Count how many items you can check. Most Delhi businesses score:

  • Below 30% (poor): Heavy SEO work needed. You're invisible in local search.
  • 30-50% (typical): You're competitive but losing to better-optimized rivals.
  • 50-75% (good): You're winning some local searches. Push to 80%+ for dominance.
  • 75%+ (excellent): Map-pack ranking likely. Compound advantage builds month over month.

FAQs

How long does local SEO take in Delhi?

2-4 weeks for initial map-pack ranking on low-competition keywords. 2-4 months for competitive Delhi-wide queries. 6+ months for sustained top-3 results in saturated categories.

How many citations do I need?

20-50 high-quality, NAP-consistent citations. Quality matters more than quantity — 30 great citations beat 200 random ones. Focus on Tier 1 (Google, Bing, Justdial, Sulekha) first.

Do reviews really matter for local SEO?

Yes — significantly. Google's local algorithm weighs review count, rating, recency, response rate, and keyword content within reviews. A business with 200 recent 4.7★ reviews beats one with 50 old 4.5★ reviews.

Can I do local SEO myself or do I need an agency?

GBP optimization, basic citations, and schema you can do yourself — it takes 20-40 hours. Ongoing content, review velocity, and citation building are where agencies add the most value. If you have time, do the basics yourself; outsource the ongoing work.
