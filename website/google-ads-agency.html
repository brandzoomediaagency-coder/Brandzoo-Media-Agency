FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take Google Ads to become profitable?

Search campaigns can be profitable within 30 days. PMax and Shopping typically need 4-6 weeks of conversion data before the algorithm becomes efficient. We design every account to have early wins so you're not paying for learning.

Is Performance Max worth it?

For e-commerce with a strong feed and decent conversion volume — absolutely. For pure lead-gen or low-volume accounts, classic Search campaigns often outperform PMax. We pick what fits your data, not what Google pushes.

What's a good Quality Score?

7+ is good, 8-10 is excellent. A 1-point QS improvement can lower your CPC by 15-30%. We work to push every active keyword to 7+ through ad copy, landing page relevance, and CTR optimization.

Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads first?

It depends on intent. Service businesses with high-intent searches (lawyers, dentists, real estate) should start with Google. D2C brands selling lifestyle products should typically start with Meta. Most accounts run both — we usually advise dominant channel first, then expand.

Do you handle YouTube Ads too?

Yes. YouTube falls under Google Ads. We run Skippable In-Stream and Demand Gen campaigns for brands with strong video creative and a real awareness/consideration play. Not every brand needs YouTube — we'll tell you honestly if it's not a fit.

Can you take over an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We start with a full audit, fix tracking gaps, restructure inefficient campaigns, and refresh ad copy. Most account takeovers see meaningful CPA improvements within 30-45 days.
