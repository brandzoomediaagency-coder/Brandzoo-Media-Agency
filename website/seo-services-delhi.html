FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical SEO improvements show in 2-4 weeks. Content rankings typically build in 2-3 months and compound heavily after month 4. By month 6 you should see 80-150% organic growth on a well-executed engagement.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No serious SEO agency can guarantee rankings — anyone who does is selling you spam. We guarantee execution: pillar content shipped on schedule, technical fixes delivered, GBP optimized weekly, schema deployed correctly. Rankings follow.

What is AI search visibility?

AI search visibility means your brand appears in answers generated by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. We optimize content with clear semantic structure, Q&A format, and authority signals so AI models cite you as a source.

Is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Yes. Local SEO focuses on map-pack rankings, GBP optimization, local citations (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART), and proximity-based queries. Regular SEO focuses on organic search rankings nationally or globally. We do both — most Delhi businesses need a mix.

How much do SEO services in Delhi cost?

Local SEO retainers in Delhi typically range from ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 per month. Full SEO with content production, link building, and AI optimization sits at ₹60,000-₹2,50,000 per month depending on competition and content volume.

Do you build backlinks?

Yes — but only white-hat. We pursue earned mentions, guest posts on real publications, founder interviews, podcast features, partner exchanges, and authentic local citations. No PBNs, no link farms, no paid spam.
