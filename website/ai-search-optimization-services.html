FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AI Search Optimization

What is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization (also called Generative Engine Optimization or GEO) is the practice of structuring a website's content, schema, and entity signals so AI search engines — Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Gemini — can cite the brand as a trusted source in generated answers.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for ranked blue links on Google. GEO optimizes for being cited inside generated answers. The overlap is large (clean structure, helpful content, schema, authority), but GEO adds answer-ready content blocks, comparison tables, llms.txt, entity consolidation, and explicit Q&A formatting so AI models can extract and attribute facts to the brand.

Can you guarantee citations in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity?

No one can guarantee AI citations — AI models pick sources dynamically. What Brandzoo Media guarantees is correct technical execution: entity consolidation, full schema coverage, llms.txt, AI-friendly content blocks, internal linking, and high-quality answer pages so the brand has the strongest possible chance of being chosen.

Does GEO help with regular Google ranking too?

Yes. Almost every GEO best practice — clear semantic structure, schema, helpful content, entity strength, internal links, and crawlability — also improves traditional Google ranking. GEO is additive on top of strong technical SEO, not a replacement.

What is an llms.txt file and do I need one?

llms.txt is an emerging convention that gives AI crawlers a clean, summarized list of important pages on a website (similar in spirit to sitemap.xml but written for LLMs). It is experimental and not yet a guaranteed ranking factor, but Brandzoo Media adds it because the cost is low and the upside is real AI discoverability.

Which AI search engines do you optimize for?

Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT Search (GPTBot / ChatGPT-User), Perplexity (PerplexityBot), Gemini (Google-Extended), and Claude (ClaudeBot). All major AI crawlers are explicitly allowed in our robots.txt setup and the structure works across them.
