Our strategy

We treat every website as a conversion + ranking machine. Information architecture, page speed, internal linking, schema, and CRO are baked in from day one — not bolted on after launch. Every page is built mobile-first, accessibility-compliant, and Core Web Vitals green.

We pair the right tech stack to your business: Shopify for e-commerce, WordPress for content-heavy sites, Next.js / Astro for static-fast marketing sites, custom React for complex apps. No 'one platform fits all'.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in India?

Marketing websites: ₹60,000-₹4,00,000. E-commerce: ₹1,50,000-₹10,00,000+. Custom builds (Next.js, React) for SaaS/B2B: ₹3,00,000-₹25,00,000+. Pricing depends on scope, integrations, and stack. We quote fixed prices, not hourly.

How long does it take to build a website?

Marketing sites: 3-5 weeks. Mid-complexity e-commerce: 6-10 weeks. Custom Next.js / headless builds: 8-16 weeks. Timelines depend on content readiness, integrations, and approval cycles.

Should I use Shopify, WordPress, or custom code?

Shopify for e-commerce (best out-of-box). WordPress for content-heavy sites or smaller budgets. Next.js / custom for performance-critical or app-like sites. We pick what fits your business stage and growth plan.

Do you handle SEO during website development?

Yes — and it's the whole point. Every site we ship has schema, sitemap, robots.txt, meta tags, OG, semantic HTML, lazy loading, optimized images, and clean URLs from day one. We also run keyword research to inform page structure.

Can you migrate my existing website to a new platform?

Yes. We've migrated sites from WordPress → Shopify, Wix → Next.js, and custom legacy to Shopify Hydrogen. Migrations include URL redirects, SEO preservation, content port, and post-launch indexation monitoring.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and updates?

Yes. Post-launch we offer monthly retainers covering bug fixes, content updates, performance monitoring, security patches, and feature additions. Standalone projects also include 30 days of free post-launch support.
