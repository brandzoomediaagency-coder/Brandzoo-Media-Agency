FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is social media marketing different from paid social ads?

Social media marketing is organic — building audience, posting content, engaging community, growing followers. Paid social (Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads) is the paid amplification layer. Both work together: organic builds trust and content library, paid scales reach and conversions.

Which platforms should my business be on?

It depends on where your audience is. D2C brands typically focus on Instagram + Reels. B2B brands focus on LinkedIn + YouTube. Local businesses prioritize Instagram + GBP + WhatsApp. We'll recommend a focused mix, not 'be everywhere'.

Do you create the content or do we?

We create everything. Strategy, scripting, monthly shoots, editing, captions, hashtags, posting. You approve. Some clients prefer to do their own shoots — we can adapt to that workflow too.

How many followers will I get in 3 months?

Follower growth is not our success metric — engagement, saves, DMs, and revenue are. That said, well-executed Instagram strategies typically deliver 30-100% follower growth in 3 months on top of meaningful business outcomes.

Do you manage influencer collaborations?

Yes. We have an in-house influencer marketing team that handles creator outreach, scripting, briefing, payment, and content rights. See our influencer marketing services.

Can social media drive direct sales?

Yes — through DM-based selling, story link-stickers, IG Shopping, and trackable UTM links. We integrate social funnels with WhatsApp and CRM so every DM-to-sale is measurable.
