FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Google Business Profile and why does it matter?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the local business listing that appears in Google Search and Maps. For local service businesses, GBP is often the highest-traffic, highest-intent customer touchpoint — even higher than your website.

How long does it take to rank in the map pack?

Map-pack rankings for low-competition local keywords can shift within 2-4 weeks of GBP optimization. Competitive keywords (e.g., 'dentist Delhi') take 2-4 months of citation building, review velocity, and content signals.

How many reviews do I need to rank?

Generally you need to be within 20-30% of the top-ranked competitor's review count, with similar recency. If your top competitor has 200 reviews, you need ~150+ at minimum to compete. We help you build review velocity ethically.

Do you handle multi-location businesses?

Yes. We've managed GBP for chains with 8-50+ locations. Multi-location work includes centralized posting, consistent NAP across all branches, and per-location performance dashboards.

Can you remove negative reviews?

We can't remove genuine negative reviews — Google won't allow it. We can request removal of reviews that violate Google's policies (spam, fake, offensive). For genuine negative reviews, we craft professional responses that often turn detractors into neutrals or promoters.

How is this different from SEO?

GBP management is a subset of local SEO. SEO targets your website's organic rankings; GBP targets the map-pack listing. Both work together — we usually bundle them for local businesses. See our SEO services in Delhi.
