Our strategy

We start with audience-product fit research — finding creators whose audience demographics, interests, and engagement patterns align with your product. Then we build a tiered campaign: nano (10K-50K) for trust + frequency, micro (50K-500K) for engagement, macro (500K+) for reach.

Every creator brief is engineered with a clear conversion goal — affiliate link, discount code, or DM-to-sales path. We negotiate usage rights upfront so winning creator content gets repurposed in your Meta Ads creative engine.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do influencers charge in India?

Nano creators (10K-50K) typically charge ₹3,000-₹15,000 per reel. Micro (50K-500K) charge ₹20,000-₹2L. Macro (500K-5M) charge ₹2L-₹15L+. Pricing varies hugely by niche, engagement rate, and exclusivity terms.

How do I measure influencer marketing ROI?

We use a combination of unique discount codes, affiliate links, UTM tracking, and cross-platform attribution (Northbeam, Triple Whale). Every campaign has a clear KPI — cost per acquisition, attributed revenue, or pipeline contribution.

Should I work with nano or macro creators?

Both — in different ratios depending on your goal. Nano creators (10K-50K) drive better engagement and trust, ideal for products needing social proof. Macro creators (500K+) drive reach and awareness. We usually recommend a 70/20/10 nano/micro/macro mix.

How long does an influencer campaign take to plan and execute?

From kickoff to first creator going live: 3-4 weeks. That includes shortlisting, outreach, contracts, content creation, approvals, and posting. Larger campaigns (20+ creators) take 5-6 weeks.

Can creator content be reused in Meta Ads?

Yes — but only if usage rights are negotiated upfront. We include 90-day or 12-month paid usage rights in every contract. This lets you turn winning creator content into top-performing UGC ads.

Do you handle creator payments and contracts?

Yes. We handle full creator management: shortlisting, negotiation, contracts, payment, content approvals, posting, and reporting. You approve the shortlist — we run the rest.
