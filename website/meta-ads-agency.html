FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on Meta Ads to start?

We recommend a minimum daily budget of ₹2,000-₹3,000 to give Meta's algorithm enough conversion volume to exit Learning Limited. Below that, the algorithm doesn't get enough signal to optimize effectively, and CPLs spike.

Are Meta Ads still worth it in 2026?

Yes. Meta still has the largest audience graph and the most mature creative-driven optimization on the planet. With Conversions API and Advantage+ Shopping, it's actually easier to scale profitably in 2026 than it was 2 years ago — provided your creative is strong.

Do you handle UGC video production for ads?

Yes. We run an in-house UGC creator network of 14+ creators across India. Scripting, briefing, filming, editing, and ad-ready delivery — all under one roof. This means we can ship a new winning ad in days, not weeks.

Will you also manage Instagram Ads?

Meta Ads cover both Facebook and Instagram (same Ads Manager). We run placements across Reels, Stories, Feed, Explore, and the broader Meta network. Instagram-only campaigns are easy to set up if your brand is Instagram-native.

How do you handle iOS 14.5+ attribution?

We install Conversions API server-side via Google Tag Manager Server or Stape, plus enhanced conversions. This restores 90%+ of attribution that was lost post-iOS 14.5. Combined with platform deduplication, your Meta numbers actually start matching Shopify again.

Can I get an account audit before signing up?

Yes. We offer a free Meta Ads audit covering account structure, tracking integrity, creative library, and 3 specific opportunities. Book it on our contact page or via WhatsApp.
