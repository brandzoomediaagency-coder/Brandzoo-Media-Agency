FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a good cost per lead (CPL)?

It depends on the industry. Real estate: ₹40-₹150. Healthcare: ₹150-₹500. EdTech: ₹100-₹400. B2B SaaS: ₹500-₹3,000. The right benchmark is whatever makes you profitable based on lifetime value (LTV) and close rate.

How do you ensure lead quality?

Three layers: (1) Targeting — we exclude low-intent audiences, junk geos, and lookalike segments that historically don't convert. (2) Landing page — we ask qualifying questions in the form. (3) Automation — WhatsApp pre-qualifies leads before they hit sales.

Do you integrate with our CRM?

Yes. We integrate with HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Freshworks, Pipedrive, and Razorpay. Leads flow in real-time with source, campaign, and ad-level attribution.

Can you handle WhatsApp lead generation?

Yes. We set up WhatsApp Business API (via Wati, AiSensy, or Interakt), build conversational flows, and automate lead qualification + routing. Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Meta typically deliver 30-50% lower CPL than form leads.

How long until I see leads flowing?

Campaigns typically go live within 7-10 days of kickoff (after audit, landing page build, and tracking setup). First leads typically arrive within 24-48 hours of campaign launch.

Do you also handle sales follow-up?

No — we generate leads, integrate them into your CRM, and notify sales. Closing is your sales team's job. We can recommend sales enablement tools and call-recording solutions if your team needs that.
